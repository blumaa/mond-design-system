`Skeleton` takes `lines`, standing in for a paragraph rather than a box.

Above one line the last is cut short, where a paragraph's last line tends to
land. A block of full-width bars reads as a table, not as prose — which is why
every app that stacked skeletons by hand ended up re-deriving the same ragged
edge. Text only: a rect or a circle is one shape however many lines of copy it
replaces.
